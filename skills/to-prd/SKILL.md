---
name: to-prd
description: Turn a product idea or conversation into a product requirements document.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Synthesize the user's product idea into a PRD. Clarify the problem, target users, goals, core user journeys, functional requirements, constraints, scope, and acceptance criteria. Keep product requirements separate from implementation details. This skill is kept as a compatibility entry for the former `to-prd` workflow; the current Matt Pocock skillset uses `to-spec` for the shipped engineering specification workflow.
