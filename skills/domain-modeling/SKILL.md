name: domain-modeling
description: Build and sharpen a project's domain model, terminology, CONTEXT.md, and ADRs.
---

Actively build and sharpen the project's domain model while designing. Challenge conflicting or fuzzy terminology, propose precise canonical terms, stress-test relationships with concrete edge cases, and cross-check stated behavior against the codebase. When a term is resolved, update `CONTEXT.md` immediately. Keep `CONTEXT.md` free of implementation details. Offer an ADR only when a decision is hard to reverse, surprising without context, and the result of a real trade-off.

Use `CONTEXT-FORMAT.md` and `ADR-FORMAT.md` in this directory for their respective formats.
