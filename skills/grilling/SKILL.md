---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea until reaching shared understanding.
---

Interview the user relentlessly until you reach a shared understanding. Map the design as a decision tree and work through the current frontier in rounds. Ask questions only for decisions; investigate environmental facts yourself. Each question should include your recommended answer. Wait for the user's answers before the next round. Do not act until the whole decision tree has been resolved and the user confirms the shared understanding.
